Post-dive logging

Controls for the conditions prompt and voice-logged dive entries.

Automatic prompt

Auto-prompt for dive conditions

When on, Shore Dive may show a quick conditions prompt (with the option to log a full voice entry) after a Safe-Return check-in or a completed dive plan.

Turning this off only disables the automatic prompt — you can still log a dive manually anytime below. It also never appears while a Safe-Return alarm may be sounding.

Voice logging

Speech-to-text Available on this device

Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded. Unsupported on some browsers (notably iPhone Safari); when it's not available, "Record voice log" is replaced with manual entry automatically.

Manual entry

Log a dive now

Open the same structured logbook entry — depth, runtime, marine life, conditions — without waiting for a prompt.

Always available, even with auto-prompt off.
